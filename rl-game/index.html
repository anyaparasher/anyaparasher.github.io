The Feed

You control a recommendation feed. Pick a post, earn clicks, and see what your choices do to trust. Then train an agent: does the score you reward lead to the outcome you actually want?

2. Choose the incentive

Defaults reward clicks alone. Change the numbers to test a different incentive. Changes restart the game and reset training so curves stay comparable.

Original: no extra reward for restoring trust.

Use a negative number to penalize trust loss. The finish bonus applies only on post 20. No loss penalty applies when trust is already low.

15 table states: last post (none / news / sports / outrage / local) × trust (low / medium / high). Trust itself has exactly three levels. The agent sees the category, but does not see the round number.

Inspect the Q-table and model

Click probabilities are fixed: news 55%, sports 65%, outrage 90%, local 40%. News and local raise trust one level; sports holds it steady; outrage lowers it one level. Trust starts high and stays between low and high. Clicks are independent of trust. The episode ends after 20 moves; terminal Q-updates have no future value. Because time is excluded from the state, this compact agent cannot tailor its policy to the final round.
